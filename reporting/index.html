What it looks like

Four situations this work is built for. Yours probably looks like one of them, or two at once.

For example

  1. A shelter running intake on paper

    A paper form, retyped into a spreadsheet, then copied into the funder template. We set it up so the monthly report comes out of intake.

  2. One program, three funders

    Three templates count the same people three different ways. We write one set of definitions and map each template to it, so the reports agree.

  3. A new platform that never got set up

    The licence is paid and staff went back to paper. We configure it around your work, then train the people who use it.

  4. A new reporting requirement coming down

    A new indicator is due next fiscal year. We find where it already lives in your records, and what to start capturing now.

Configuration and training partner for a case management platform

We hold a signed agreement with the vendor of a case management platform for mental health and social service agencies, to configure it for the agencies that use it. We set it up, and we train your staff so it actually sticks.

    Software shaped like that, built on top of what you already have. Nothing gets ripped out to make room.

  4. Keeping it working

    Operations change, funders change their reporting, staff turn over. Someone who knows how it was built has to be there.

  1. Leadership meeting

    To ensure the vision and alignment.

  2. Week one

    Collecting info, having meetings with people, checking the whole landscape, gathering documents and materials. At the end of the first week, the initial plan and process map.

Data

Five spreadsheets and five sites become one live dashboard. The numbers you already collect start answering the questions leadership and funders ask.

For example

  1. Five sites, five spreadsheets

    Each site counts the same work its own way. We agree on one definition per number and connect every sheet to one dashboard that stays current.

  2. The numbers only you have

    Wait times, where referrals come from, what people ask for that nobody offers yet. We turn what only you hold into indicators a funder can read.

  3. Seeing a change the month it starts

    If walk-ins double over three weeks, the dashboard shows it in week three, not in the annual report. You can act on it the same month.

  4. A directory people trust

    Services in a region, kept current by the staff who run them, so a worker finds the right door on the first call. See it in the community hub portal.

Strategy

Plan, budget and staff hours become one workplan you can track.

For example

  1. A strategic plan on a shelf

    The plan has five goals and none of them shows up in this month's work. We break each one into owners, hours and cost.

  2. A budget that does not match the hours

    The budget funds one full-time role and the timesheets show half of it. We line up budget and hours so you see the gap before a funder does.

  3. A funding call with a deadline

    The call closes in four weeks. We turn the work you already do into the workplan, budget lines and indicators the application asks for.

  4. Two organizations applying together

    One shared workplan: who delivers what, who reports what, and where the money goes. Written so both boards can sign it.
