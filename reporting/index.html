Four things happen, in this order.

Every build runs the same way. You see the plan before a line is written, and someone stays after it ships.

  1. Research and consultation

    It starts with a conversation about how the work actually runs. Where it stalls, and which spreadsheet is quietly holding it together.

  3. Building

The two weeks

  1. Leadership meeting

    To ensure the vision and alignment.

  2. Week one

    Collecting info, having meetings with people, checking the whole landscape, gathering documents and materials. At the end of the first week, the initial plan and process map.

  3. Week two

    We review that for feedback with leadership, and each stakeholder responsible for certain parts reviews those. We take all of that feedback, adapt, and then you have a final product with any minor adjustments.
