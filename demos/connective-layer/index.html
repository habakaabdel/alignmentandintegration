      Signed in as Program lead, Millbrook Housing Collective. You own these listings. Change one and the regional search shows it right away.

        Change log

          What it is, and is not

          Nothing replaced. Nothing moved.

          It does

          • Give staff one search across a region
          • Put a named owner on every referral step
          • Let staff keep their own listings current
          • Send short notices through the email you already use

          It does not

          • Replace your records, scheduling, payroll or email
          • Store personal health information
          • Move referrals out of each hub's own records
          • Ask anyone to learn a new system to keep working

          Every agency, town, service, phone number and date on these screens is invented for this demonstration.
