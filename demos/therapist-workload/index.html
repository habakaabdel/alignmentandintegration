Therapist workload

One board for a therapist working two sites. What you owe, to whom, by when, and what it counts toward. Every client on it is a coded card: a nickname, an avatar, a number. Who they are stays in your head, not in here.

What to try

  1. Open the 11:00 session. The board pulls what you need from that one card.
  2. The finished 9:00 session is asking for its recap. Dictate it, confirm the items it pulled out, and watch the transcript go.
  3. Open the caseload and tap Boat. The map holds only what bears on the reason for counselling. His spirit column is empty because nothing there is doing work yet.
  4. One row is red. Only one thing on the board needs you to act, so only one thing is red.
  5. Open hours. The sessions on the board post themselves against the college’s 450 and 100. Confirm today and watch both meters move. Nothing on that tab is a client’s information.

Sample caseload, fictional throughout. Tuesday, March 10, 2026 on the board. Nothing you do here is stored.

Built by Alignment Integration on its engine. Sample data. No names, no notes, no recordings kept: dictation is read once for its action items and discarded. The hours tab holds the therapist's own professional record and no client information at all.
