For community and social services

demo live

Diagnostic and intervention map

Four domains read at four levels, from the person out to the society around them. Sixteen cells, each holding its own diagnostic questions and intervention moves.

The connections between them carry direction and weight, so the map shows where acting changes the most. The reading comes back as a written report.

Open the map

demo live

Community hub portal

One portal for each hub, and connective tissue between them. A quick search across the services in a region, the referral pathway from one agency to the next, and a registry the staff keep current themselves.

Fictional agencies and fictional data throughout. Nothing here stores a person's health information.

Open the portal

demo live

Staff portal over a clinical record

The day's work in front of the worker, built over a clinical records system rather than inside it. The record stays where it is. Fictional agency, fictional data.

Open the portal

demo live

Community safety and well-being portal

Built for a community safety partnership, where police, health and social services sit at one table. It holds the situation table record: who was brought forward, which agencies took a part, and what happened after.

Referrals move between partners inside the portal instead of over the phone. Fictional municipality, fictional agencies, fictional data.
