Personal Pal: Aura

An adaptive personal assistant for your pocket. Talk to it to capture thoughts, routine updates, or schedule changes. It updates your life map, extracts action items, and shreds the raw transcript to protect your cognitive privacy.

What to try

  1. Choose a scenario below in the simulator controls to dictate a life update.
  2. Observe the real-time transcription as Aura listens.
  3. Review the structured items Aura pulls out (rescheduling, task additions, or energy shifts).
  4. Tap "Keep items and shred raw input" to commit the adjustments and watch the raw recording dissolve.
  5. Navigate between today's timeline and your life map tabs to see the updates applied.
