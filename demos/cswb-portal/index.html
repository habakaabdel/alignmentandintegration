    About The Plan

    Background, development timeline, and the contact for the fictional City of Alderport Community Safety and Well-Being Plan.

    Municipalities are required to develop a Community Safety and Well-Being Plan. Alderport's plan, covering a ten year horizon, was released after a period of community consultation and data collection. It sets out 7 priority areas, 15 recommendations, and 48 actions aimed at strengthening safety and well-being across the community.

    A plan document on its own is difficult for a resident to act on. This portal puts the two useful halves in one place: the services a person can call today, and honest progress on what the municipality committed to. Both halves sit on the same page, so nobody has to know which department owns which piece.

    Development Timeline

    • Year 0, autumn Project start
    • Years 0 to 1 Data collection and community engagement
    • Year 1, spring Plan released
