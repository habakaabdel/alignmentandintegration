What it does

A product you would use, not a system we build for your clinic. Different shape, same engine underneath.

  1. one

    It carries what you owe

    Sessions coming up, reports due, the thing you promised to bring on Thursday. In time order, each one opening to what it needs.

  2. two

    It holds no names

    Every client is a coded card: a nickname, an avatar, a number if you want one. Which card is which person exists in your head and nowhere else.

  3. three

    The map is fenced to the concern

    Each card carries a weighted map of what bears on the reason for counselling, and only that. No life history, no intake. A domain stays empty until something in it matters.

  4. four

    Dictate the recap, keep only the items

    After a session, say what happened while it is fresh. It pulls out the action items, you confirm them, and the recording and transcript are discarded. Your case notes stay yours, wherever you keep them.

Therapist workload

A working prototype you can open now. It runs on a sample caseload and exists to show the shape of the product. The full build follows the conversation, not the other way around.

Open the prototype

What we are not claiming: this is not a clinical records system and it is not built to be one. It stores no client names, no session notes, and no recordings, so it does not stand in for the documentation your college requires. Session content stays in your own case notes.
