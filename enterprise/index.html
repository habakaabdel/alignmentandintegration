01

What we build at this scale

A different sales motion and a different build, but the same engine underneath as every other line.

  1. one

    One description of the operation that survives the trip upstairs

    What is happening across every location, in terms both ends recognise. The value is not another dashboard. It is that the floor and the head office stop arguing about whose number is right before they can talk about the problem.

  2. two

    It sits on top of what you already run

    Large operations have already bought their systems and are not replacing them. We build on the layer above, reading what those systems already produce. Nothing gets ripped out to make room.

  3. three

    Comparison across sites that means something

    Sites record the same thing differently, so comparing them naively produces confident nonsense. Before anything is aggregated we map how each site actually captures each measure, so what gets summed means the same thing everywhere. That mapping is most of the work and it is usually skipped.

  4. four

    Research first, in the operation

    We start by understanding how the work runs, on site, from the people doing it. Then a plan you see before a line is written. The method is not sector-bound, which is the whole positioning: the same approach has been applied in retail abroad and in health and social services here.
