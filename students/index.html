a packaged product, not a build

Every course, every deadline, every note, in one app you talk to.

Your material is scattered across a learning portal, a downloads folder, three notebooks and your inbox. The work of studying is mostly the work of finding things again.

What it does

This one is a product you would use, not a system we build for your institution. Different shape, same engine underneath.

  1. one

    Ask it what is due

    Hold the mic and ask. Every course, every deadline, in the order they are actually coming at you.

  2. two

    It answers from your material

    A general assistant tells you what the literature says. Yours tells you what your professor assigned.

  3. three

    Make it quiz you

    Before an exam, on your own material, in the format you will be tested in. The study method with the most evidence behind it and the least follow-through.

  4. four

    It keeps up as the term moves

    Readings get added, a deadline shifts, a course changes shape halfway through. What you saved in September is still easy to find in December.
