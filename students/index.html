a packaged product, not a build

Your outlines already hold the whole term. This pulls it out.

An outline buries the term in a grading table, a weekly schedule, and a sentence halfway down the page. Five outlines bury it five times over. Paste them in and get one list, earliest first, with what each item is worth.

What it does

This one is a product you would use, not a system we build for your institution. Different shape, same engine underneath.

  1. one

    Paste the outline

    One course or all of them at once. The grading table, the due dates and the weekly schedule are the parts that carry the term.

  2. two

    It reads rather than guesses

    Every graded or dated line becomes one item, with its title, its type, its due date, and what it is worth.

  3. three

    One order, and a table per course

    Items from every course merge into a single timeline, earliest first. Each course also keeps its own table, so you can see where the grade actually sits.

  4. four

    It flags instead of filling in

    Weights that do not add to a hundred, items with no date, wording that could mean two things. Those come back as questions rather than invented answers.

What we are not claiming: this does not write your assignments and it is not built to. It organizes what your outline already says, and where the outline says nothing it tells you so rather than filling the gap. Check the result against the outline your instructor gave you.
