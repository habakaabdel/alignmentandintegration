The process map, before and after

People understood the mess because they lived in it.

Before, as it runs today

14 steps

4 loops. The same fact in 5 places.

  1. Quarter ends. The funder template arrives by email.
  2. Data lead exports the client list.
  3. Exports service contacts and joins the two in a spreadsheet.
  4. Four supervisors fill caseload sheets from memory and paper.
  5. Supervisors email their sheets to the data lead.
  6. Data lead reconciles. The totals do not match.
  7. Emails each worker about missing fields.
  8. Workers fix some records. Data lead exports again.
  9. Clients in two programs are de-duplicated by hand.
  10. Reception tallies walk-ins from a paper binder.
  11. Data lead types the indicators into the funder template.
  12. Finance checks units against targets. They do not line up.
  13. Executive director questions a number.
  14. Director signs. Copies saved to the shared drive.

The five crossed-out steps are deleted outright.

After, the same report

8 steps

1 loop. Each fact in 1 place.

  1. In the quarter: intake and discharge fields are required pick lists.
  2. In the quarter: reception logs walk-ins and calls as they happen.
  3. Quarter ends. The funder template arrives by email.
  4. Data lead runs the indicator report. One client, one count.
  5. The report lists records with a missing field.
  6. Supervisors clear them within two days.
  7. Data lead copies the indicators into the funder template.
  8. Finance checks units. Director signs, finance submits.

We build within your systems or on top of them. You do not change systems.
