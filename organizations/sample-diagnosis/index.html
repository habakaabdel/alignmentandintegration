Sample diagnosis, on fictional data

You map out the full process, whatever it is that we're automating or improving, and through that you can identify in each step what's required and how the whole workflow will be done.

offer
Diagnosis and intervention. Two weeks, $3,500 CAD flat.
setting
A fictional 40-staff community mental health and addictions agency in Ontario.
process
The quarterly report to a funder: indicators pulled from records and spreadsheets.
reader
Executive directors and finance officers.

01

The process map, before and after

People understood the mess because they lived in it.

Sometimes you don't even need AI or automation; it's just simplifying, because you may have multiple steps in the process that don't need to be there, multiple sources, too many exception cases. Once you can map it all out you can really optimize it.

We will build within your systems or on top of your systems, and not have you change systems.

03

What you receive

Once you can map all that out, it becomes easy to automate, to improve, to optimize.

A process map

  • Before: every step, loop, exception case, and place the same fact lives.
  • After: the redesigned process, indicators captured once in the records system.

The initial plan

  • What to delete, what to change in the records system, in what order.
  • The three measures, before and as planned: time, data accuracy, cost.

The two weeks

  1. Leadership meeting

    A leadership meeting to ensure the vision and alignment.

  2. Week one

    The first week would be collecting info, having meetings with people, checking the whole landscape, gathering documents and materials.

    At the end of the first week, we develop the initial plan and process map.

  3. Review

    Review that for feedback with leadership, and then each stakeholder that's responsible for certain parts will review those.

  4. Final product

    We take all of that feedback, adapt, and then have a final product with any minor adjustments.

AI is not a miracle solution that will do everything you want.
