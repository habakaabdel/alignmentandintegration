What we build for a small operation

Small does not mean simple. It means one person holds several jobs at once and the system has to survive that.

  1. one

    One board instead of four tabs

    Covers, shifts, stock, bookings, no-shows. The numbers your day turns on, on one screen, readable on a phone with one hand free.

  2. two

    Built on how your shop already works

    Your booking system, your supplier, your spreadsheet stay where they are. The software wraps what exists instead of replacing it.

  3. three

    The quiet questions, answered

    Which nights make money once staffing is counted, which no-show pattern costs you a shift, which stock is going missing. Your records already hold the answers.

  4. four

    Someone there when it changes

    Software is not furniture. You add a location, a supplier changes format, a staff member leaves and takes the process with them.

How it works underneath

The same engine runs every build we do. Three ideas cover it. The longer version is on the home page.

one
It captures what happens as it happens, so nobody has to remember Tuesday on Friday afternoon.
two
Overnight it separates the noise from what mattered, so the morning read is short.
three
You govern what becomes the record. Nothing is filed as fact on its own.

What we are not claiming: this is not an accounting system and it does not replace your point of sale. We will not promise you savings before we have seen how your shop runs. The initial consultation is free, and it is a conversation, not a sales call.
