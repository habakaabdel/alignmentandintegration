What we build for a small operation

Small does not mean simple. It means one person is holding several jobs at once and the system has to survive that.

  1. one

    One board instead of four tabs

    Covers, shifts, stock, bookings, no-shows. The handful of numbers your day turns on, on one screen, readable on a phone behind the counter with one hand free.

  2. two

    Built on how your shop already works

    We do not arrive with a process for you to adopt. Your booking system, your supplier, your spreadsheet stay where they are. The software wraps what exists rather than replacing it, because a small operation cannot afford a month of everyone learning a new way to do the same job.

  3. three

    The quiet questions answered before you ask them

    Which nights actually make money once staffing is counted. Which no-show pattern is costing you a shift. What stock is walking. These are answerable from what you already record, and mostly nobody has the time to sit down and answer them.

  4. four

    Someone there when it changes

    You take on a second location, a supplier changes their format, a staff member leaves and takes the process with them. Software is not furniture. Someone who knows how it was built has to be around when the shape of the work moves.

How it works underneath

The same engine runs every build we do. Three ideas cover it. The longer version is on the home page.

one
It captures what happens as it happens, so nobody has to remember Tuesday on Friday afternoon.
two
Overnight it separates the noise from what mattered, so what you read in the morning is short.
three
You govern what becomes true. Nothing is filed as fact on its own say-so.

What we are not claiming: this is not an accounting system and it does not replace your point of sale. We do not quote a return before we have looked at your operation, and we do not put a number on this page that we have not measured. The initial consultation is free and it is a conversation about how your shop runs, not a sales call with slides.
