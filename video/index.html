What it looks like

Short videos for the people you want to reach, shot on a phone and cut the way they watch. People follow people, so a real face goes in front wherever it helps.

For example

  1. An Airbnb that needs more bookings

    A short for social and a full tour for the listing, like the Wolfe Retreat video above. The hook lands on the first frame.

  2. A restaurant with a new menu

    We film the dishes, the kitchen and the people who cook. One shoot becomes a month of short posts.

  3. A service showing people how to get help

    Where to go, what happens at intake, who you will meet. Your own staff on camera, because people trust a face they will see again.

  4. An owner who should be on camera

    A monthly package built around you. We plan the topics, film them in one sitting and cut them into shorts that go out through the month.

  5. A league or an event that wants highlights

    Every basket in order with a running score, cut to the music you pick.

How it works

Script, shoot and edit become one finished walkthrough.

  1. A 30-minute call, then a proposal

    What the video is for, who needs to see it, and where it will be posted.

  2. Script and shot list

    Every line and every shot written down before anyone films, so the shoot day stays short.

  3. The shoot

    On a phone, at your place, with you or your staff in front of the camera when it helps.

  4. The edit

    Cut to how people watch: the hook first, captions on, music under it. Video includes two rounds of revisions.

  5. Ready to post

    Each video comes in the size each place needs: vertical for Reels, TikTok and Shorts, wide for your website.
