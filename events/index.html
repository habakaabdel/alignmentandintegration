Events

MC, activities, sign-in data and screens become one night that runs smoothly. People come for each other, and we take care of everything else.

Contact us What you can buy

What you can buy

Events are quoted one at a time.

Book one piece or the whole night. Each one works on its own, and they fit together.

  1. MC an event or run the night

    We welcome the room, keep the program on time and handle the hand-offs between speakers, so the night ends when you said it would.

  2. Coordinate the event

    Venue, vendors, volunteers and the run sheet in one plan everyone can see.

  3. Set up activities

    Icebreakers, team challenges, a tournament bracket, games for the kids. Each activity has a goal, so people leave knowing someone new.

  4. Data collection

    Sign-in on a tablet or a QR code, not a clipboard. You get the attendance count and the feedback when the night ends, and the data stays yours.

  5. The software so it runs smoothly

    Registration, check-in and the schedule on people's phones, set up and tested before the doors open.

  6. Screen animations as people walk in

    Your logo, the sponsors, the agenda and a welcome on the big screen as people walk in, timed to the program.

What it looks like

People want to be with people.

For example

  1. A community gathering for families

    Sign-in at the door, an MC for the program, activities for the kids, and an attendance count for your funder before you leave.

  2. A league finals night

    The bracket on the big screen, an MC calling the games, and a highlight video of the night for everyone who played.

  3. A monthly meet-up that keeps people coming back

    Same night, same format, one shared goal. Sign-in remembers who came, and everyone gets a reminder before the next one.

  4. A staff training day

    The agenda on the screen, activities between sessions, and feedback collected before people reach their cars.

How it works

A 30-minute call, then a proposal. A 50 percent deposit to start.

  1. A 30-minute call, then a proposal

    Events are quoted one at a time.

  2. A 50 percent deposit to start

    The deposit holds the date. Then we plan the run of show with you and set up the sign-in, the software and the screens.

  3. The night

    We arrive early, test everything and run the night. When it ends, the sign-in data goes to you.
