Alignment Integration / community and social services

health, social services, coordinated care

The work between agencies is where people get lost.

Every agency knows its own part. What nobody holds is the space in between: who has the next step, what was already tried, and whether the referral that went out three weeks ago landed anywhere. This is the work we know from the inside.

01

What we build for this sector

Written for agencies delivering services to people and drowning in the coordination around delivery, rather than in the delivery itself.

  1. one

    A view of the pathway, not just your part of it

    Where a person sits across the services touching them, who is accountable for the next step, and what happens in the handoff. The gap between agencies is not a data problem for any one of them, which is exactly why it stays unowned.

  2. two

    Reporting that comes out of the work

    Funder reporting is usually reconstructed at quarter end from memory and spreadsheets, by the people who can least afford the afternoon. If the record is made while the work happens, the report is a read of what is already there rather than a separate job.

  3. three

    A map of the whole person, in the sector's own language

    One way of reading a system: its layers, the pieces in each, how much each matters, and how they connect. Applied to a person, that is the biopsychosocial and spiritual reading practitioners already work in. The reasoning stays in plain language, so you can read it, question it, and correct it in your own words when you think it is wrong.

  4. four

    Consent and governance treated as the design, not the paperwork

    Who can see what, under whose consent, and what happens when it is withdrawn. In this sector that question decides whether a system can exist at all, so it belongs at the start rather than in a compliance review afterwards.

02

How it works underneath

The same engine runs every build we do. Three ideas cover it. The longer version is on the home page.

one
It captures what happens as it happens, so the record is not written afterwards from memory.
two
Overnight it separates the noise from what mattered, so the morning read is short.
three
A person with authority over the work governs what becomes canon. That is the whole governance model, and it is deliberately boring.

What we are not claiming, and this matters most in this sector: this is not an electronic medical record and it does not replace one. We do not hold client health data. We do not publish outcome numbers we have not measured, and we will not put a percentage on a slide because it makes the case land better. Where a build would touch personal health information, that is a consent and governance conversation before it is a software conversation. The initial consultation is free.
