03

How it works

The engine that runs client builds. Three ideas cover it.

  1. one

    It captures what happens as it happens

    The work leaves a record while it is being done. Nobody has to remember Tuesday on Friday afternoon.

  2. two

    It works out overnight what is worth keeping

    Between one day and the next, the noise gets separated from what mattered. What you read in the morning is short.

  3. three

    A person governs what becomes the record

    Nothing is filed as true on its own. Someone with authority over the work confirms it, and that confirmation is the record.
