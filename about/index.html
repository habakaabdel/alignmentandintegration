Person

Co-constructing meaning, and finishing the job.

I'm able to translate and integrate. That's my real skill: I'm able to think in all different levels and talk in all different ways. I'm able to sit with the minister of mental health and addictions and then also sit with the frontline case manager, and connect with both.

More about me

How I work

I am great at co-constructing meaning, letting you be the expert. My intention is making the job complete while also ensuring it's done in a good way for all stakeholders involved. Through meaning, alignment, and integration, all parties involved can win together.

I bring complexity to simplicity and chaos to order, and I can provide different perspectives. I stop the disconnection and bring in alignment and integration across anything. With AI, I now can integrate software too.

If there's things that I'm doing repetitively, those can be automatic, with my final review on them.

Where the perspective comes from

Born in Saudi Arabia, moving to Canada in my elementary years and really experiencing Canada for those foundational years, then moving back to Saudi Arabia during a more restrictive way of life. My own family on my dad's side are not wealthy; they're farmers in Egypt, in a poor economy.

I have lived alone in multiple different environments, been impoverished, and straddled cultures.

I do a lot of effective work through my walking and conversation.

Agency · Kenora Chiefs Advisory, 2025 to 2026

One record system, 24 programs, reporting that runs from the work

No standard data collection across the agency. Every funder report was a day's work, or multiple days. When a person leaves, a whole system may collapse. No integration or communication across programs without it being manual.

Read the case

What I did

I took all of that to setting up an electronic medical record for the programs, aligned with data reporting, and created a staff portal for data accuracy and training.

The configuration of the record itself fits with every program's needs. Programs that have specific reporting, it's now being captured and can be easily identified in a report that's generated with two clicks, rather than filtering through Excel sheets and building databases on the side.

Role and scope

  • Led the implementation across 24 behavioural health and social wellness programs, replacing the previous client record system.
  • Reporting configuration and data definitions: mine.
  • Staff portal: software I built myself, in use behind the organization's sign-in since August 2026.
