How we work

A 30-minute call, then a proposal. A 50 percent deposit to start.

  1. A 30-minute call, then a proposal

    The initial consultation is free. It is a conversation about your situation or your work, and you get a plan out of it whether or not you build anything with us.

  2. A 50 percent deposit to start

    Building with you and not for you. I am great at co-constructing meaning, letting you be the expert.

  3. The work

    I do a lot of effective work through my walking and conversation. I am a good speaker. I ask what each party needs to move forward. Through open discussion where the emotional energy is managed, we get to a solution that gives all parties their demands.

  4. Finishing the job

    My intention is making the job complete while also ensuring it's done in a good way for all stakeholders involved. Through meaning, alignment, and integration, all parties involved can win together.
