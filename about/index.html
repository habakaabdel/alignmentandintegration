How we work

A 30-minute call, then a proposal. A 50 percent deposit to start.

  1. A 30-minute call, then a proposal

    The initial consultation is free. It is a conversation about your situation or your work, and you get a plan out of it whether or not you build anything with us.

  2. Building with you, not for you

    We are great at co-constructing meaning, letting you be the expert.

  3. The work

    We do a lot of effective work through our walking and conversation. We are good speakers. We ask what each party needs to move forward. Through open discussion where the emotional energy is managed, we get to a solution that gives all parties their demands.

  4. Finishing the job

    Our intention is making the job complete while also ensuring it's done in a good way for all stakeholders involved. Through meaning, alignment, and integration, all parties involved can win together.
