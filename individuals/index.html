01

What you might bring

None of these is a problem to be fixed. Each one is a situation with parts that need to line up.

  1. one

    A goal that needs a plan

    Where you want to get to is clear. The order of steps, and what has to give, is not.

  2. two

    Competing demands

    Work, family, study, health, money. Each one reasonable on its own, and together they do not fit in a week.

  3. three

    A big decision or a transition

    A move, a change of work, a new stage of life. What it changes downstream matters as much as the choice itself.

  4. four

    Barriers to progress

    Something keeps getting in the way. Naming it accurately is most of the work.

  5. five

    Too many moving parts

    You want someone to think it through with you, on the whole picture at once.

02

What happens

It starts with the whole situation, not one piece of it.

Understand the whole situation
Your priorities, responsibilities, and circumstances, and how each one affects the others.
Clarify what matters
Which things are load-bearing, which can wait, and what you are not willing to trade.
Connect the pieces
A practical response that fits your life and your culture, in an order you can actually follow.

What we are not claiming: this is consultation and planning. There is no personal tool or software built today. If the work calls for one, we say so and build it with you, on top of what you already use.
