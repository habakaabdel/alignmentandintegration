The thought in the car, the thing someone told you in a hallway, the decision made standing up. By the time you can write it down, it is gone.

What it is meant to do

Written as intent rather than features, because this one is not built yet.

  1. one

    Talk instead of typing

    Hands busy, walking, driving, mid-conversation. If capture needs you to sit down and type, the detail worth keeping never gets kept.

  2. two

    It sorts what you give it

    You should not have to decide where something goes the moment you think of it. Filing is what kills every note-taking system.

  3. three

    It answers from your own material

    Ask what you decided about something in March and get your answer, not a general one.

  4. four

    You govern what becomes true

    Nothing is filed as fact on its own. When it gets something wrong you say so, and the correction is the record.
