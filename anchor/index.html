Alignment Integration / Anchor

Anchor puts one agreed exercise on your client’s phone between sessions.

Licences are not on sale yet.

Open the Anchor page

How it works

  1. You set one exercise

    Pick a starter exercise or write your own, then show your client a code to scan.

  2. Your client tries it, or taps Not Today

    During the week they log Tried It or Not Today, and can note what got in the way.

  3. They bring back one page

    Before the next session your client picks one entry and chooses which details go on the page.

What Anchor never does

  • It never asks your client to make an account.

  • It never sends an entry to a server. Entries stay on your client’s phone.

  • No one watches entries between sessions, you included.
