Reference Check, privacy

What Reference Check receives, and what happens to it

Reference Check is a plugin for ChatGPT published by Alignment Integration, a one person consultancy based in Mississauga, Ontario. It checks references against Crossref. This page covers the plugin only. The rest of this site is covered by the site privacy notice. Last updated 2 October 2026.

What it receives

When you ask ChatGPT to check references, ChatGPT sends the plugin the text of those references and any DOIs in them. That is all it receives. There is no sign up, no account, and nothing is gathered in the background.

  • Reference text. The references you asked to have checked, as written. A reference names the authors of a published work, so those names are part of what is sent.
  • Server records. Our host keeps standard technical logs of requests, including IP addresses, for security and reliability, and counts requests from each address to limit how fast the service can be called. We do not use those logs to build a picture of who you are.

The plugin does not ask for your name, your email address, or anything about you, and it has no use for them. Please do not paste personal or confidential material into a reference list.

Why it receives it

To look each reference up and report whether Crossref holds a matching record. That is the whole list. None of it is used for advertising or to profile anyone.

Where it goes

The plugin runs on Netlify. To look a reference up, it sends the reference text or the DOI to Crossref, the registry that holds the records, at api.crossref.org. Crossref handles those requests under its own privacy policy, which is published at crossref.org. Each request to Crossref carries our contact address, not yours.

Netlify and Crossref are based in the United States, so what is sent is processed there and is subject to the laws of that country.

Your conversation with ChatGPT, including the results the plugin returns, is held by OpenAI under OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy. We do not see your conversation.

What is kept

Nothing. The plugin has no database and writes nothing down. A reference is held only for the seconds it takes to look it up, and is gone when the answer is returned. The host's technical logs are kept by Netlify for the period Netlify sets.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell information, and we never will.
  • We do not share what is sent with anyone beyond the services named above.
  • The plugin sets no cookies and runs no tracking.
  • The plugin shows no advertising and does not promote any service.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law, PIPEDA, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy of it, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. Because the plugin stores nothing, there will usually be nothing to give you, and we will tell you so plainly.

Write to alignmentandintegration@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. There is no form to fill in and no fee. If you are not satisfied with how we handle it, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Children

The plugin is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
